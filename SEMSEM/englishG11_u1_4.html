Question tags 2

Main Idea

Some special cases in Question Tags include imperatives, "I am", indefinite pronouns, and modals.

بعض الحالات الخاصة في استخدام علامات الاستفهام تشمل: الأوامر، الضمير "I am"، الضمائر غير المحددة، والأفعال الناقصة.

Imperatives (الأوامر)

When using commands or requests, we can use "will you?" or "won’t you?" as tags.

  • Close the door, will you?
  • Help me, won’t you?

عند استخدام الأوامر أو الطلبات، نستخدم "will you?" أو "won’t you?"

"I am" in Question Tags

When the sentence starts with "I am", the tag is "aren’t I?" (not "amn't I").

  • I am your friend, aren’t I?

مع "I am" نستخدم "aren’t I?" بدلاً من "amn't I?" لأنها غير صحيحة.

Indefinite Pronouns

For words like "someone", "everyone", "nobody", we usually use "they" in the tag.

  • Everyone is here, aren’t they?
  • Nobody called you, did they?

نستخدم "they" مع الضمائر غير المحددة مثل everyone, someone, nobody.

Modal Verbs

Use the same modal verb in the tag.

  • She can drive, can’t she?
  • They shouldn’t be late, should they?
  • We must leave now, mustn’t we?

مع الأفعال الناقصة نستخدم نفس الفعل في علامة الاستفهام.

Summary

Question Tags follow special rules with imperatives, the verb "to be", pronouns, and modal verbs. Always pay attention to the meaning and the verb type.

Special Cases Summary

  • I am → aren’t I?
  • Everyone/Nobody → they
  • Imperatives → will you? / won’t you?
  • Modals → use the same modal (can, must, should…)

Practice Questions

  • Let’s go, shall we?
  • Don’t be late, will you?
  • He must leave, mustn’t he?

Tips

  • Look at the **main verb** and **subject** to choose the correct tag.
  • Question tags help in speaking for confirmation and politeness.
