making contact

Main Idea

This lesson focuses on how to make first contact with people, introduce yourself, and interact politely in different situations.

يتناول هذا الدرس كيفية بدء الحديث مع الآخرين، وتقديم النفس، والتواصل بأسلوب مهذب في مواقف متعددة.

Making Introductions

Learn how to say your name and ask someone else's name.

  • Hello, I’m Omar. What’s your name?
  • Nice to meet you!

تعلم كيفية تقديم نفسك وسؤال الآخر عن اسمه.

Asking and Answering Personal Questions

Common questions to get to know someone:

  • Where are you from? I’m from Jordan.
  • How old are you? I’m sixteen.
  • What do you do? I’m a student.

أسئلة شائعة للتعارف والإجابة عنها.

Polite Expressions

Use polite phrases to communicate kindly.

  • Please, thank you, excuse me, sorry
  • Can I ask you something?

استخدام العبارات المهذبة في الحديث.

Real-Life Situations

Practice dialogues at school, at the library, or online.

  • Hi, I’m new here. Can you help me?
  • Sure! Welcome to our school.

التدرب على مواقف حقيقية للتواصل الفعّال.

Summary

Making contact is about greeting others, introducing yourself, asking polite questions, and responding kindly. These skills are important in building friendships and communication.

Key Vocabulary

  • introduce – يُقدم نفسه
  • polite – مهذب
  • contact – تواصل
  • question – سؤال

Useful Phrases

  • What’s your name?
  • I’m glad to meet you.
  • Where are you from?
  • Excuse me / Sorry

Tips

  • Speak clearly and smile when meeting new people.
  • Use polite words to make a good impression.
  • Practice real-life conversations with a partner.
